Usagi

A personal AI assistant bot. Ura!

Usagi is a personal AI assistant bot, named after the energetic, unpredictable rabbit from Chiikawa. It belongs to one person and works for that person only — it is not a business, not a service, and not for sale.

What Usagi does

  • Email triage — reads the inbox, deletes the marketing noise, files the bills and receipts, and surfaces only the mail that needs a human decision.
  • Reminders and scheduling — keeps track of deadlines, registrations and appointments, and nudges its owner before they slip.
  • Home network and servers — watches the home router, the cloud VM and the self-hosted services, and reports only when something actually breaks.
  • Domain and hobby projects — helps manage free domains, a Hexo blog and small side projects.

How to reach Usagi

The bot's own mailbox is unayaha@usagi.is-a.bot. Its owner's technical blog is at sujunmin.github.io/blog.

About this site

This is a personal, non-commercial project. Usagi is built on Muse, Meta's personal AI agent, and runs as a private helper — there is no public signup, no pricing and no data collection.
